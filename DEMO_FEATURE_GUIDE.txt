  ================================================================================
  GENIX DRIVE — INTERACTIVE DEMO WALKTHROUGH
  Feature Context Document
================================================================================
  
OVERVIEW
--------
The Interactive Demo Walkthrough (/demo-walkthrough) is a 43-step guided tour
of the Genix Drive mobile app. It is split into 7 feature sections, each
covering a core capability of the platform. The demo is designed to be used on
the marketing website to educate prospective users before they download the app.

Navigation:
  - Tap / click anywhere on the phone image  →  advance to the next step
  - "Previous" button in the instruction card →  go back one step
  - "Next" button                             →  advance (alternative to tapping image)
  - Progress dots (5)                         →  visual indicator of current section
  - "Restart" button on the final step        →  return to step 1

Implementation files:
  src/pages/website/DemoWalkthrough.tsx   — component (all 43 steps)
  public/demo/step01.png … step43.png     — phone mockup screenshots
  src/routes/index.tsx                    — route: /demo-walkthrough


================================================================================
  SECTION 1 — DASHBOARD  (Steps 1–2)
================================================================================

PURPOSE
  Introduces the user to the main  and the time-filter ribbon.

STEPS
  Step 1 — Welcome to Genix Drive
    Screen : Dashboard with drive score (60) and "30 Days" tab highlighted
    Action : Tap the "30 Days" filter tab
    Message: By default a 7-day filter is applied. Tap 30 days to see the
             statistics for the last 30 days.

  Step 2 — Great!
    Screen : Dashboard with drive score (40) and "365 Days" tab highlighted
    Action : Tap the "365 Days" filter tab
    Message: Now tap 365 days to view data for the entire year. Notice how
             the statistics change as you select different filters.

KEY CONCEPT
  The time-filter ribbon (7 Days / 30 Days / 365 Days) is available throughout
  the app on all stats screens; this is the user's first introduction to it.


================================================================================
  SECTION 2 — SCORE & INSIGHTS  (Steps 3–10)
================================================================================

PURPOSE
  Shows users how to navigate into the detailed driving score breakdown and
  interpret the various charts and statistics.

STEPS
  Step 3 — INSIGHTS: Click the score circle
    Screen : Dashboard with "20" drive score circle highlighted (red border)
    Action : Tap the score circle to open the inner Score page

  Step 4 — INSIGHTS: Your Score overview
    Screen : "Your Score" page with line graph
    Message: Filter at the top lets you select 7 / 30 / 365 days. The line
             graph compares your score against the average of other users.

  Step 5 — INSIGHTS: First pie chart
    Screen : "Your Score" scrolled down; first pie chart (327.22) highlighted
    Action : Tap the first pie chart
    Message: Click the first pie chart on your dashboard; the inner page
             will appear.

  Step 6 — INSIGHTS: Drive Time chart
    Screen : Drive Time chart (peak / off-peak / night hours breakdown)
    Message: Great! Look at the chart — driving hours divided into peak,
             off-peak, and night hours. Select range from the ribbon on top.

  Step 7 — INSIGHTS: Stats beside first pie chart
    Screen : Back on "Your Score"; stats 327.22 / 55.23 highlighted
    Action : Tap the stats beside the first pie chart
    Message: Go back, then click the stats located beside the first pie chart.

  Step 8 — INSIGHTS: Distance graph
    Screen : Distance graph showing distance vs other drivers
    Message: Graph shows distance covered by a number of drivers. The dotted
             line shows where you fall. Numeric values show how much you have
             traveled vs other users.

  Step 9 — INSIGHTS: Last pie chart
    Screen : Back on "Your Score"; "403" pie chart (last) highlighted
    Action : Tap the last pie chart
    Message: Go back to main insight screen, then click the last pie chart —
             the events screen will appear.

  Step 10 — INSIGHTS: Events screen
    Screen : Acceleration / Braking / Cornering events breakdown
    Message: Chart shows the number of events during the selected period and
             compares them against the average of other users.

KEY CONCEPTS
  - Three pie chart categories: Drive Time, Distance, Events
  - Each opens a dedicated drill-down screen
  - All screens support the 7 / 30 / 365 day filter


================================================================================
  SECTION 3 — CONNECTIONS: COMPANIONS & FRIENDS  (Steps 11–18)
================================================================================

PURPOSE
  Walks the user through adding family members and friends to track their
  locations and driving on a shared map.

STEPS
  Step 11 — ADD A CONNECTION: Navigate to Connections
    Screen : Map + Companions & Friends page; bottom nav "Companions" highlighted
    Action : Tap the Connections icon in the app's bottom navigation

  Step 12 — ADD A CONNECTION: Companions tab
    Screen : Companions & Friends page; "Companions" tab highlighted
    Action : Tap the Companion tab at the bottom of the section
    Message: A new screen displays the list of your existing connections.

  Step 13 — ADD A CONNECTION: Add a Companion
    Screen : "Add a Companion" search screen
    Message: Tap "Add a Companion", type the name in the search bar, and
             select the desired companion from the results.

  Step 14 — ADD A CONNECTION: Companion request confirmation
    Screen : Request confirmation dialog
    Message: Once selected, a confirmation screen appears. Tap to send the
             request.

  Step 15 — ADD A CONNECTION: Friends tab
    Screen : Back on Companions & Friends; "Friends" tab highlighted
    Action : Tap the Friends tab
    Message: The screen displays the list of your friends.

  Step 16 — ADD A CONNECTION: Add a Friend
    Screen : "Add a Friend" search screen
    Message: Tap "Add a Friend", enter the name in the search bar, select
             the friend from the results.

  Step 17 — ADD A CONNECTION: Friend request confirmation
    Screen : Request confirmation dialog
    Message: Finally, a confirmation screen appears. Tap to send — your
             friend request will be sent successfully.

  Step 18 — ADD A CONNECTION: Live location on map
    Screen : Map screen with friend/companion pin visible
    Message: Once the request is accepted, they appear on the Map screen.
             You can view their live location directly on the map.

KEY CONCEPTS
  - Companions and Friends are separate lists (different privacy/sharing levels)
  - Accepted connections appear as pins on the shared map
  - Live location sharing begins once the request is accepted


================================================================================
  SECTION 4 — POINTS OF INTEREST (POI)  (Steps 19–27)
================================================================================

PURPOSE
  Teaches users how to create named locations (POIs) associated with
  companions, with configurable entry/exit monitoring.

STEPS
  Step 19 — ADD A POI: Navigate to Places via Menu
    Screen : Side-menu open; "Places" item highlighted
    Action : Tap the top-left menu icon → select Places
    Message: Next, tap on My Places to see all saved locations.

  Step 20 — ADD A POI: My Places list
    Screen : "My Places" list with saved locations
    Message: Tap any place from the list to open the map view with a pin icon.

  Step 21 — ADD A POI: Add a Place button
    Screen : Map view with purple "Add New Place" button highlighted at top
    Action : Tap "Add New Place"
    Message: You can search for a location and set the radius for your place.

  Step 22 — ADD A POI: Radius circle
    Screen : Map showing a circular radius overlay for the new POI
    Message: A popup will appear asking for the details needed to create the
             point of interest (POI).

  Step 23 — ADD A POI: Add Companion to POI
    Screen : "Add Place" modal — Add Companions field
    Message: First, add the companion you want associated with this POI.

  Step 24 — ADD A POI: Name & icon
    Screen : "Add Place" modal — Name and icon fields filled
    Message: Enter a name for the POI and select an icon to represent it
             on the map.

  Step 25 — ADD A POI: Activity type
    Screen : "Add Place" modal — activity type selector (Exit / Enter / Both)
    Message: Choose the type of activity: Exit, Enter, or Both, to monitor
             when your companion interacts with this location.

  Step 26 — ADD A POI: Confirm
    Screen : "Add Place" modal — all fields complete, Confirm button visible
    Message: Finally, tap Confirm.

  Step 27 — ADD A POI: POI on map
    Screen : Map displaying the new POI with the selected icon
    Message: The map will display your new POI with the selected icon,
             showing its exact location.

KEY CONCEPTS
  - POIs are always linked to a specific companion
  - Three monitoring modes: Enter, Exit, or Both
  - The radius defines the geofence boundary for trigger events
  - POIs appear as custom icons on the shared map


================================================================================
  SECTION 5 — TRIPS  (Steps 28–30)
================================================================================

PURPOSE
  Shows users how to review their driving history, both as a quick summary
  on the dashboard and as a detailed per-trip breakdown.

STEPS
  Step 28 — TRIPS: Recent Trips on Dashboard
    Screen : Dashboard scrolled down; "Recent Trips" card highlighted
    Message: Scroll down on the Dashboard to see the Recent Trips section.
             Swipe right to browse through up to five recent trips.

  Step 29 — TRIPS: Past Trips list
    Screen : "Past Trips" screen with date-filtered trip list
    Message: Full list of complete trips for the selected date range. Change
             the date filter to see trips in a specific period. Tap the first
             trip to see details.

  Step 30 — TRIPS: Trip Details screen
    Screen : Trip detail view with map, score, and event markers
    Message: View the trip map, total distance covered, driving score, and
             all events that occurred. Tap event icons on the map to see the
             exact location where each event occurred.

KEY CONCEPTS
  - Recent Trips widget on dashboard shows the 5 most recent trips
  - Full history available via Past Trips with date-range filtering
  - Each trip stores: route map, distance, score, and driving events


================================================================================
  SECTION 6 — GENIX MEET  (Steps 31–41)
================================================================================

PURPOSE
  Demonstrates the social meetup feature — scheduling group meetups, inviting
  participants, real-time location tracking during the meetup, and a race mode.

STEPS
  Step 31 — GENIX MEET: Open Meets from Menu
    Screen : Side-menu open; "Genix Meet" item highlighted
    Action : Tap menu icon → open Meets section

  Step 32 — GENIX MEET: Upcoming Meets
    Screen : GenixMeet — Upcoming tab
    Message: View all meets scheduled in the future.

  Step 33 — GENIX MEET: In Progress tab
    Screen : GenixMeet — "In Progress" tab highlighted
    Action : Tap "In Progress" tab
    Message: Shows list of meets currently in progress.

  Step 34 — GENIX MEET: Completed tab
    Screen : GenixMeet — "Completed" tab highlighted
    Action : Tap "Completed" tab
    Message: Displays all completed meets. Navigate freely between
             Upcoming, In Progress, and Completed.

  Step 35 — GENIX MEET: Create New Meetup form
    Screen : "New Meetup" creation form
    Message: Tap "Create New Meetup" to see the list of details required
             to set up your meetup.

  Step 36 — GENIX MEET: Select Location
    Screen : "Select Location" map screen
    Message: Drag on the map to choose the meetup location.

  Step 37 — GENIX MEET: Select Participants
    Screen : Participants list screen
    Message: A list of potential participants you can invite to the meetup.

  Step 38 — GENIX MEET: Send Invites
    Screen : "Meetup Mapper Invite" confirmation popup
    Message: Tap "Send Invite" — participants will be notified on their end.

  Step 39 — GENIX MEET: Chat Screen
    Screen : In-meetup chat screen with message history
    Message: Message participants, share updates, or coordinate during
             the meetup.

  Step 40 — GENIX MEET: Map view
    Screen : Meetup Map tab showing real-time participant locations
    Action : Tap the Map icon/tab
    Message: Shows real-time locations of all participants on the map.

  Step 41 — GENIX MEET: Race mode
    Screen : Meetup Race tab with participant rankings
    Action : Tap the Race option/tab
    Message: The Race Screen shows participant positions in the race or
             activity. See who is leading in real-time.

KEY CONCEPTS
  - Three meetup states: Upcoming / In Progress / Completed
  - Meetup setup flow: Name → Location → Participants → Send Invite
  - During a meetup: Chat, Map (live locations), and Race tabs are available
  - Race mode shows a real-time leaderboard of participants


================================================================================
  SECTION 7 — REWARDS  (Steps 42–43)
================================================================================

PURPOSE
  Introduces the rewards/loyalty system where users earn points from safe
  driving and redeem them for real-world prizes.

STEPS
  Step 42 — REWARDS: Available Rewards
    Screen : Rewards screen — "Available" tab with food/product rewards
    Message: First screen displays all Available Rewards you can claim.
             Swipe or scroll to see the variety of rewards you can earn.

  Step 43 — REWARDS: Purchased Rewards
    Screen : Rewards screen — "Purchased" tab highlighted
    Action : Tap the "Purchased" tab
    Message: Shows all rewards you have already claimed or purchased,
             so you can keep track of your collection.

KEY CONCEPTS
  - Rewards are earned through safe driving behavior and high drive scores
  - Available tab = claimable rewards
  - Purchased tab = collection of already-claimed rewards
